About me

Short version: I make things clear before I make them pretty.

I'm a graphic designer based in Karachi. I started designing in 2024 and joined Supertech LEDs in July of that year, where I design social media content, logos, banners and email layouts, and prepare the artwork for the signs the company builds.

I'm comfortable taking a brief from a rough idea, showing options early, and handing over clean files. If you also need someone who can talk to a production team without a translator in between, that's me.
